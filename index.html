About

Rev. Femi Ibikunle has given his life to ministry, to leadership, and to bringing people of different faiths together.

Born on the 1st of September in Ibadan, Oyo State, he has spent years serving the church and the public. His leadership began early, as a Royal Rangers Commandant and as an Executive Member of the Assemblies of God Nigeria Youth Department. He has also held several roles in Oyo State and national youth councils.

Today he works for religious harmony, peace and understanding in his role as Special Assistant on Interfaith Affairs to the Executive Governor of Oyo State. He is an international peace advocate, known across the world for his commitment to peacebuilding and understanding between cultures.
